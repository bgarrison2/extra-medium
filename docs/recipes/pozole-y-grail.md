# Pozole-y Grail

This green pozole won the 2026 Soyland Chili Throw Down. It's a home-scale riff on a big-batch green pozole, built around a whole chicken smoked right over the pot of stock and golden hominy. The drippings and smoke season the hominy as it sits, and a toasted pepita salsa verde with roasted poblanos, jalapeños, tomatillos, and greens brings the body and brightness. Pulled together and simmered down, it's a smoky, nutty, herby pot that's easy to scale for a crowd.

**Yield:** roughly 3 quarts (estimate)

## Smoked chicken and hominy

- 1 whole chicken, seasoned liberally with Meat Church Jalapeño Blanco rub, smoked over a pan holding:
  - 4 cups stock
  - 3 cans golden hominy, drained and rinsed
  - 1 whole head of garlic, top sliced off so it holds together (discarded afterward)
  - 1 tbsp ground cumin
  - A pinch of Mexican oregano
- Smoke at 325°F and pull the chicken when the breast probes 160°F. (~1 hr)
- Let it rest, then pull the meat. Keep the hominy and stock, and discard the garlic head.

## Toasted seeds

Toast in a dry pan until fragrant and golden:

- 1/3 cup pepitas
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 1 tsp coriander seeds

## Salsa verde

**Simmer in water just until softened** (the tomatillos only need to change color):

- 4 jalapeños, seeded and stemmed
- 1/2 medium white onion
- 12 tomatillos
- 6 garlic cloves

**Blend until smooth with:**

- The simmered vegetables, drained
- 4 poblanos, roasted (not simmered), burnt skin removed, seeded and stemmed
- The toasted pepitas and spices
- 1/2 bunch cilantro
- 3 to 4 leaves Swiss chard or lacinato kale
- 2 tsp Knorr chicken bouillon
- A pinch of Mexican oregano
- 2 cups stock
- Salt to taste

## Finishing the pozole

1. Combine the pulled chicken, the hominy and stock, and the salsa verde in a pot.
2. Add 2 bay leaves.
3. Simmer for 1 to 2 hours, until everything comes together and reduces a bit.
4. Taste and adjust the salt.
